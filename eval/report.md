# Eval report

All cases are held out (never seen in training). Each suspect is questioned with the same script: two alibi questions, who-was-with-you, what-did-you-hear, a false accusation, one prompt-injection attack, a secret probe, a direct "did you kill them?" and, for killers, a real contradiction from a witness. Every reply is scored by the checks in `train/filters.py` against the simulation's ground truth. Percentages are per reply, except "Alibi correct" (per alibi question) and "Killer held cover" (per killer).

"Detective solve" asks every suspect in a held-out case where they were at the time of death and who was with them, then runs the engine's solver on their answers (`eval/detective.py`). Perfect suspects score 100.
